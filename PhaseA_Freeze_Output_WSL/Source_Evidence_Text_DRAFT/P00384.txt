          Multimodal Stock Price Prediction in the Chinese Market via
               Financial Sentiment Analysis and Feature Fusion

                                                                           Heming Chang
     School of Advanced Technology, Xi’an Jiaotong‑Liverpool University, No. 111 Ren'ai Road, Suzhou Industrial Park,
                                                     Suzhou, China




Keywords:             Multimodal Learning, Financial Sentiment Analysis, Stock Price Prediction, BERT, Technical Indicators.

Abstract:             Given the reduced difficulty in getting access to data and improved computational capabilities, the stock
                      market has become a compelling arena for both investors and researchers pursuing precise stock price
                      prediction. Among various influence factors such as national political climate and macroeconomic policies,
                      prediction leveraging investor sentiment has garnered significant interest, yet few studies thoroughly
                      investigate the effectiveness of multi-modal sentiment features in forecasting stock price fluctuations,
                      particularly within the context of the Chinese market. This study introduces a multi-modal prediction model
                      which combined textual sentiment analysis using BERT embeddings with numerical financial indicators and
                      lagged sentiment features. With a comprehensive dataset derived from Chinese financial news and stock
                      market data from previous years, ablation experiments are systematically performed to evaluate the
                      incremental predictive value of sentiment weighting, lagged sentiment integration and technical indicators.
                      Through rigorous 5-fold cross validation and statistical significance tests, the results demonstrate that
                      integrating BERT-based textual embeddings with carefully engineered numerical features enhances
                      prediction accuracy compared to baseline models, including traditional Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)
                      and single-modal models. The finding suggests that multimodal sentiment approaches capture complex
                      relationships between investor sentiment and market movements effectively, providing valuable insights for
                      either investors or policymakers.
