                   II. LITERATURE REVIEW
    One of the most crucial and significant tasks in text
mining, NLP, and data retrieval is text preprocessing [12].
Unorganized information is prepared for information
extraction using this method. Text preparation involves a
wide range of activities; some of the most popular methods
include stemming, tokenization, and stop-word reduction.
    A study [13] suggested a strategy for predicting the
Indonesian share market using sentiment evaluation in
tweets. The approach aims to forecast price fluctuations,
margin percentages, and share value movements. Tweet
estimations were made using five supervised categorization
methods: SVM, NB, DT, RF, and NN. The investigation
found that RF and NB models functioned better than other
methods, with an accuracy of 60.4% and 56.6%,
respectively. LR is effective at predicting prices with 67.8%
accuracy. The research's shortcoming is that the prediction
system is solely dependent on five prior days' values.
    The study [14] examined how financial news affected
daily variations in index fluctuations and the forecasting of
share values. The goal of this research is to increase forecast
accuracy by integrating rule-based classification with
technological evaluation. The monthly mean for the
everyday shares and financial news are the two main
sources of information for the forecasting system. [15]
offered a framework that uses the analysis of non-
quantifiable data from news stories to forecast market
changes. This framework incorporates an NLP approach
utilizing the numerical variable-based component and
SentiWordNet 0.3. The model produced either favorable or
adverse conduct and word polarity based on the share's
fundamentals at entry and exit.                                                          Fig.1. The suggested paradigm for stock trend estimation

competent in analyzing financial terms, potential sentiment,
and even sarcasm. Such models would be probable to
enhance the accuracy of the classification of the financial
news, once used in the sentiment analysis stage, and the
predictive behavior of the overall system would improve the
overall framework. This would be a major step into
developing more intelligent, context aware, financial
forecasting systems.
